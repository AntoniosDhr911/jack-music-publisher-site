Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

JACK Music Publisher is a desktop application currently in private development and testing. It helps authorized users prepare and publish creator-owned media to supported social media platforms.

Information used

The application may access basic account information and authorization data provided by connected platforms when necessary to authenticate the user and perform user-requested publishing actions.

Authentication data

Authentication credentials and tokens used by JACK Music Publisher are stored locally on the user's device and are used only to communicate with the connected platform on behalf of the authorized user.

Content

Media and publishing information are processed only as necessary to carry out actions explicitly requested by the user.

Data sharing

JACK Music Publisher does not sell personal information. Information is shared with connected social media platforms only when required to perform an authorized publishing or authentication operation.

Third-party services

Use of connected services such as TikTok is also subject to the privacy policies and terms of those services.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application develops.
